New

  • The main window can now be made much smaller. The minimum size dropped from 800×600 to about 360 wide, roughly phone width, so Lite Gemini fits as a narrow side window, especially with Always on Top on (Settings → General). At narrow widths Gemini switches to its compact layout. New installs still open at 800×600, and existing windows keep their current size.

Notes

  • The app is not notarized, so macOS blocks it on first launch. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, or run: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Lite Gemini.app"
  • After updating, macOS may ask for microphone permission again. This is expected with the current signing setup; grant it once per update.
